What VOD-engine is

VOD-engine is a self-hosted tool, run privately by a VOD channel manager (the "operator"). It prepares a streamer's past broadcasts and, with the channel owner's permission, uploads them as private videos to their YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube Terms of Service

VOD-engine uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to VOD-engine, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and you acknowledge the Google Privacy Policy.

Your permission

Connecting a channel lets VOD-engine do what is described in the Privacy Policy: read the channel's name, upload private videos, and delete private drafts it uploaded itself. It never publishes videos or changes their visibility. You can withdraw this permission at any time on the Google security settings page.

Content

The channel owner remains responsible for the content on their channel, and for it complying with the YouTube Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The operator reviews every upload before it is published.
