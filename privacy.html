Privacy Policy

VOD-engine · Last updated 29 September 2026

VOD-engine is a self-hosted tool, run privately by a VOD channel manager (the "operator"), that prepares a streamer's past broadcasts and uploads them, as private videos, to a YouTube channel whose owner has connected it. This policy explains what data VOD-engine accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how you can revoke access or have data deleted.

YouTube API Services

VOD-engine uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel to VOD-engine, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What VOD-engine accesses

When a channel owner connects their channel, they grant VOD-engine these Google permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to upload videos to the channel;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube: to read the channel's name, and to delete private drafts that VOD-engine itself uploaded when a corrected version replaces them. (When the owner connects using a code at google.com/device, Google only offers this permission, and it also covers uploading.)

Using these, VOD-engine only:

  • reads the connected channel's name and ID, to check that it is the intended channel;
  • uploads videos as private, with a title and description written by the operator;
  • receives the video ID of each video it uploads;
  • deletes only videos it uploaded itself, and only when replacing a draft.

It does not access your email address or Google profile. It does not read analytics, comments, subscribers, messages or any other videos, and it never publishes or changes the visibility of videos.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the operator's own self-hosted server, and are used only for the actions above. They are kept until you revoke access or the operator disconnects the channel. VOD-engine checks them every day, and a token that no longer works is deleted immediately.
  • The channel name is refreshed from YouTube every day.
  • Video IDs of uploads are kept while a video is being prepared, and deleted no later than 30 days after work on that video is finished.
  • Data obtained through the YouTube API Services is never sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and is never used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

VOD-engine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Recordings (not YouTube data)

The stream recordings VOD-engine works on are supplied directly by the streamer, not obtained from YouTube. To prepare them, short audio clips may be sent to Shazam to identify copyrighted music, and speech transcripts may be sent to an AI model through OpenRouter to find where the VOD should start and end. No data obtained through the YouTube API Services is sent to these services.

Cookies, device data and third parties

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. VOD-engine's own control panel is private to the operator and may set a single sign-in cookie in the operator's browser. VOD-engine does not collect data from, or store data on, the devices of channel owners, and it does not show ads or third-party content.

Revoking access

You can revoke VOD-engine's access to your data at any time on the Google security settings page (also reachable from your Google Account → Third-party access). Once access is revoked, VOD-engine can no longer use your channel, and its stored token is deleted within a day.

Deleting your data

To have all data VOD-engine holds about you or your channel deleted, email hi@swalsh.me. It will be deleted within 7 days, and you will be told when it's done.

Contact

Questions or complaints: hi@swalsh.me.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Material changes will be sent directly to the owners of connected channels.
