Privacy policy

VOD-engine · Last updated 29 September 2026

VOD-engine is a private, self-hosted tool used only by its owner.

YouTube

VOD-engine uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

It only uploads private videos, reads the channel name, and deletes videos it uploaded itself.

Your data

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Google data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI training.

Revoking access

Revoke access at any time in your Google Account. Stored tokens are deleted on request.
