Privacy policy

VOD-engine · Last updated 29 September 2026

VOD-engine is self-hosted software used by a single operator to manage the livestream VOD channels they run. It has no public users. This policy explains what it does with data from the services it connects to.

YouTube API Services

VOD-engine uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

With the access you grant, VOD-engine:

  • uploads videos to the connected channel as private videos, with a title, description and category;
  • reads the channel's name, to show which channel is connected;
  • deletes earlier uploads of the same video that VOD-engine itself made, when a corrected version replaces them.

It does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments, analytics or account information.

What is stored

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens for the connected channel, stored in a file on the operator's own computer.
  • The channel name, and the IDs of videos VOD-engine uploaded, in a local database on the same computer.

Nothing is stored on any server run by VOD-engine; there is none.

Sharing

Data obtained from Google or YouTube is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or to train AI models.

Separately from Google data, VOD-engine uses these services for its own processing:

  • Twitch: to read the public title, games and timing of the operator's streams.
  • OpenRouter: excerpts of the stream's speech transcript (generated locally) are sent to an AI model to find where the intro and outro are. No Google or YouTube data is included.
  • Discord: status notifications are sent to the operator by direct message.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke VOD-engine's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services. Disconnecting the channel in VOD-engine, or revoking access, stops all further use; the stored tokens are deleted on request, and are unusable once access is revoked.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue at github.com/SwultCode/vod-engine-site.
