VOD-engine

A self-hosted tool for preparing a streamer's past broadcasts and uploading them to a VOD channel on YouTube.

Who uses it

VOD-engine is run privately by a VOD channel manager on their own server. It is not a public service and has no sign-ups. The only people who interact with it are that operator and the owners of the channels it uploads to, who each connect their own channel themselves.

What it does

  • Takes the streamer's own recording of a livestream, compresses it, trims it to the part meant for the VOD channel, and helps the operator remove copyrighted music.
  • With the channel owner's permission, uploads the result to that channel as a private video. It never publishes videos: the operator reviews each upload in YouTube Studio and publishes it by hand.
  • Reads the connected channel's name, to make sure it is uploading to the right channel.
  • Deletes older private drafts that it uploaded itself when a corrected version replaces them.

YouTube API Services

VOD-engine uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and to VOD-engine's Terms of Use. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what data it accesses and stores, and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke its access at any time in your Google security settings.
